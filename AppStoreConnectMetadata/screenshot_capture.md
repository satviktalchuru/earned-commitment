# Screenshot Capture Plan

Screenshots must be captured from the signed app on a real iPhone or an installed iOS Simulator runtime. Do not use mockups or fabricated images.

Capture these states:

1. First-open guide with the local-only message.
2. Today screen with the score and empty state.
3. Commitment creation screen with a clear deadline.
4. Active commitment detail screen.
5. Missed state with recovery action.
6. Settings screen showing privacy and research links.

Use an iPhone 6.9-inch simulator or device where possible. Upload PNG/JPEG screenshots without transparency. The first three screenshots should explain the core experience without relying on long text.
