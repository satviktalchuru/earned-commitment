# Privacy Answers Draft

The app is local-only. It has no account, server, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking, or third-party data sharing.

Proposed App Privacy answer: no data collected, subject to confirming Apple's definition in App Store Connect and verifying the release build has no data leaving the device.

The privacy manifest declares UserDefaults access with reason CA92.1. This is a platform compliance declaration and is separate from the public privacy policy.
