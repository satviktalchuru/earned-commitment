Earned Commitment helps you follow through on the goals you choose for yourself.

Make one specific commitment, choose a real deadline, and confirm completion yourself. When a commitment is missed, the app records it clearly and gives you a small recovery path instead of pretending nothing happened.

The app is intentionally private and simple:

- No account or login
- Goals and history stay on your iPhone
- Clear deadlines with no hidden grace period
- Self-confirmation instead of photo, file, or social proof
- Recovery steps after a missed commitment
- Optional local reminders
- Export and delete controls for local data
- Short explanations of the behavioral research behind the design

Earned Commitment is a personal productivity tool. It does not provide medical, financial, employment, or mental-health advice, and it does not guarantee that a goal will be completed.
