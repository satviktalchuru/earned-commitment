# Earned Commitment Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 31, 2026**

Public version: https://satviktalchuru.github.io/earned-commitment-privacy/
Support: https://satviktalchuru.github.io/earned-commitment-privacy/support.html

## Summary

Earned Commitment works without an account. Goals, confirmations, score, recovery state, history, and app preferences are stored locally on the user's iPhone. The app does not operate a server for these features and does not sell personal information.

## Information stored on the device

The app stores goal details, deadlines, commitment state, self-confirmations, score history, recovery actions, and preferences such as whether onboarding has been shown. This information is used only to provide the app's features.

## Notifications

If permission is granted, the app schedules local notifications for reminders and state changes. Permission can be changed in iOS Settings. Notifications are prompts and are not the source of truth for goal state.

## Sharing and tracking

Earned Commitment does not require login, include advertising, use analytics or tracking SDKs, or share goal data with us or third parties.

## Export and deletion

Users can export local data from History and delete the app's stored data from the same area. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data according to iOS behavior.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. Before release, the hosted version must include the publisher's support contact and a public URL entered in App Store Connect.
