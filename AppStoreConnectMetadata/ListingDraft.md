## Subtitle

## Promotional text

Turn one meaningful intention into a clear commitment with a deadline, self-confirmation, and an honest recovery path.

## Description

Earned Commitment helps you follow through on the goals you choose for yourself.

Make one specific commitment, choose a real deadline, and confirm completion yourself. When a commitment is missed, the app records it clearly and gives you a small recovery path instead of pretending nothing happened.

The app is intentionally private and simple:

- No account or login
- Goals and history stay on your iPhone
- Clear deadlines with no hidden grace period
- Self-confirmation instead of photo, file, or social proof
- Recovery steps after a missed commitment
- Optional local reminders
- Export and delete controls for local data
- Short explanations of the behavioral research behind the design

Earned Commitment is a personal tool for making commitments more concrete. It is not medical, financial, employment, or mental-health advice, and it does not guarantee that a goal will be completed.

## App Review notes draft

Earned Commitment does not require an account. All goal and history data is stored locally on the test device. On first launch, follow the brief guide, create a commitment, and use the self-confirmation flow. Notifications are optional. Export and deletion are available from History. To test a missed deadline, create a goal with a near-term deadline and relaunch after it expires.
